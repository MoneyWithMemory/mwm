# Founder Context

## About Your Business
- **Company name**: Money with Memory (MwM)
- **Industry**: Bitcoin / RGB digital assets, local-first AI agents, P2P trading
- **Target audience**: Bitcoin/RGB-native traders and OTC desks who want private P2P swaps without custodians or cloud AI leaking their limits (early adopters, privacy-conscious power users)
- **Value proposition**: Local AI negotiates, RGB settles, you stay in control. Private reservation prices never leave the device; the human approves every settlement (2/2 sign-off).

## Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Technical, trustworthy, privacy-first
- **Key messages**: "Local AI negotiates. RGB settles. You stay in control."; no custodian, no cloud LLM, no autonomous spending

## Business Goals
- **Short-term (3-6 months)**: Working negotiation + approval + RGB settlement demo (hackathon, Torino 17-18 Oct 2026); testnet pilot
- **Long-term (1-3 years)**: Reference non-custodial agentic OTC layer on RGB/Bitcoin, with counterparty memory and stablecoin (Tether WDK) settlement
- **Key metrics**: completed swaps, negotiation-to-settlement rate, spread vs. mid-price, leak-guardrail violations (target 0)

## Products/Services
- **Main offerings**: Local bilateral AI negotiation agent (QVAC Qwen3-4B), deterministic bounds checks, counterparty memory, approval UI/API, RGB on-chain swap settlement
- **Pricing model**: Not defined (see BUSINESS_PLAN.md)

## Competitors
- **Main competitors**: Centralized OTC desks, DEX/AMM and order-book platforms, Lightning/Taproot Assets and other RGB swap tooling, cloud-LLM trading agents
- **Your advantages**: Privacy of limits, non-custodial settlement, human-in-the-loop by design
